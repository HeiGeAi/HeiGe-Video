# 100-video mechanism index

A source-linked research index, not a ranking. 79 mechanism references, 3 case-study/tutorial references, 1 mixed reference and 17 controls are retained with their limitations. All 100 works received newly inspected decoded source samples; 51 were in the old baseline and 49 are new. Full-timeline sampling is uneven, especially for long films. Selected action intervals were inspected more densely. No full-speed playback, listening, model-provenance verification or asset-reuse clearance is implied.

Use the [small reference selector](../scripts/select_references.py) to load a few relevant cases. The [machine-readable ledger](research-cases.json) retains exact coverage timestamps, source hashes, rights and caveats. External media is not distributed.
