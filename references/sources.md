These instructions independently summarize reusable methods. They do not bundle third-party demo footage, frames, music, characters, brand assets or proprietary fonts. Source links identify research; linking a reference is not permission to copy its contents.

The supplied review records source-code reading and actual sampled-frame inspection separately. ## Asset ledger for each new film

For every external asset record origin URL, author/rightsholder if known, exact file/hash, license or user authorization, attribution obligation, modifications and whether redistribution is permitted. For factual sources record retrieval date, relevant claim and evidence location. Unknown rights mean research-only until permission is established.

A public download, a permissively licensed engine and a private reference review are three different things. Keep research evidence private unless sharing is separately authorized; distribute original deliverables and only the assets cleared for that use.
