# Swiss-tech director pack

Status: revised original example has a continuous technical pass and targeted actual-frame acceptance. Its formerly generic path now uses a concrete two-note relation, while remaining a conceptual knowledge demonstration rather than verified real-product behavior. Method references: [Swiss style](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/styles/swiss-motion/STYLE.md), [motion implementation](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/blob/main/styles/swiss-motion/demo/ease.js), [reference film](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/swiss-motion.mp4).

## World and media

The world is a flat printed composition whose grid is a structural coordinate system. Type, rules and solid modules share a baseline and module scale. A technology topic does not require a glowing HUD or imitation software screen. Choose the number of columns from the content's scale; many fine columns can accidentally read as a chart.

## Object identity

Track the same word, letterform, module or content record through rearrangement. Use a stable ID and a preassigned destination. A label becoming a node must preserve its meaning and source; changing shape must not silently change the fact it represents. Establish the grid before one meaningful exception breaks it.

## Camera

Use flat translation, controlled zoom, or a deliberate quarter-turn of the designed surface. Settle a rotation before introducing fresh reading text. A close view makes one typographic operation understandable; a pullback reveals the result as a system. Do not add fake perspective to every frame.

## Actions and consequences

Legal primitives include clip-reveal, reflow, align, split along an existing rule, join, rotate a whole composition, cut and hold. Motion uses monotone easing or linear interpolation. NO springs, rebound, overshoot, perpetual floating or elasticity. A beat is the arrival time: start the travel early enough to land on it.

A signature action must change a meaningful state. For a source-linked answer, make the selected question trace to an identifiable source passage and carry that evidence into an answer; a generic network of dots cannot prove retrieval. For a pure poster film, a spacing or hierarchy change must visibly improve or disrupt the same composition.

## Transitions

Let outgoing modules become incoming structure. A crop window can reveal text, or a large rule can become the next composition's axis. Preserve IDs during reflow. A hard cut is allowed when it communicates a real chapter change; repeating the same central title with new copy is not a complete visual story.

## Material

Use flat opaque ink, restrained paper/ground, one signal accent and subordinate construction rules. Build weight with large masses and spacing, not shadows, bloom, bevels or particles. Texture is optional and should not defeat the print logic. Optical alignment may differ from mathematical bounding boxes.

## Text

Type is an object, not a permanent top caption. Use one coherent family with contrasting weight/size where available. Large letterforms may be intentionally clipped as image; essential reading text must become complete and stable in its reading window. Check final glyphs and line breaks at delivery size. Keep reference labels subordinate to the action.

## Audio

Short dry clicks, restrained percussive contacts and rhythm can mark exact arrivals. Sync sound to the actual reflow/knife/landing event, not an unrelated beat grid. Give the settled result breathing room. Music and narration are optional; silence is allowed.

## Forbidden cases and checks

- A generic “snappy” spring on any moving object: inspect extrema and near-arrival frames for overshoot
- Numbers incrementing without a factual source or state change: verify the data or label an example
- Decorative empty graph: require a visible operation, accountable edge meaning and consequential result
- Titles added while the substrate is rotating: inspect reading-window boundaries
- Every shot with the same silhouette and scale: compare start, operation, result and ending thumbnails
- Fine-line clutter mistaken for sophistication: review at phone width and inspect the dominant mass first

Evidence should show the same IDs before/after the signature operation, close-up typography, transition neighbors and at least one settled reading frame.
