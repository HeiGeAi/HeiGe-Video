# What v3 changes

The design target is a stronger visible event, not a larger style menu. The v2 examples often changed labels or switched between nearby diagram layouts. The strongest sampled references preserve meaningful objects, let an action change their state, then reveal an ending that explains the opening.

## Implemented in the candidate

- Absolute-time Canvas source metadata: authored duration, readiness, optional state evaluator, declared glyph strings and hard cuts
- Original shared cue, monotone easing, analytic spring, camera, seeded-noise and arc-length stroke helpers
- Optional cut-aware, linear-light shutter sampling; encoded-video dense action/cut extraction with exact time mapping and review status
- Portable source-linked research corpus: 100 works plus 100 canonical projects; controls excluded by default from a small lexical selector
- A workflow that designs the ending, proves the signature action in actual motion, then builds the film and repairs observed defects

A workflow requirement is not an automated detector. Readability, causality, composition, media rights and aesthetic quality still need judgment.

## Transfer a mechanism, not a film

### Editorial: make a designed artifact gain a second function

[Five Rules for a Poster](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/swiss-motion.mp4) edits one poster before reusing its geometry as rhythm. Apply the idea by starting with a complete original artifact. Let alignment, spacing or grouping perform an observable edit; use the resulting geometry again in the ending. Keep typography readable in a settled window. A recolored title card does not transfer this mechanism.

### Product: preserve the things being improved

[Room to Think](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/dark-keynote.mp4) makes a sweep reorganize the same file/message/photo classes. Model records with stable IDs, typed destinations and visible content, then show the trigger and resulting interface. A conceptual product must stay labelled conceptual; animated counters cannot prove performance.

### Ink: change the threat itself

[The Swordsman and the River](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/ink-wash.mp4) separates threat, preparation, action and consequence. Transfer its causal rhythm into a new story: a shared river or wind skeleton drives shape, wet edges and local splashes; the main gesture changes that same geometry. One blurred silhouette plus whole-screen grain is not a material system.

### Ending: design the global arrangement first

[Airbnb Aircover by BUCK](https://buck.co/work/airbnb-aircover) reveals that local scenes belong to one final composition. Plan the global geometry before close-ups, then use the pullback to disclose meaning. More camera movement is not automatically better; it must reveal information.

### Data and whiteboard: perform the reasoning visibly

[A Hundred Summers](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/dataviz.mp4) changes encoding through readable intermediate states. Preserve keys and values while moving marks from one representation to another. For a whiteboard explanation, let a measuring tool, occluder or comparison produce evidence on the persistent board. Additional labels are not an experiment.

## Repairs found in actual candidate renders

These concrete defects were repaired and received scoped independent decoded-pixel rechecks in the final five-example candidate. Exact source/media hashes and remaining limitations are in [the versioned inventory](v3-example-status.json):

- Editorial ending: a headline crossed the moving paper for about 0.7 seconds. Tie the headline reveal to the paper clearing its target column, rather than a loosely chosen global fade time
- Product light: a child ray draw reset `globalAlpha`, leaking beam/rainbow before its parent card and prism existed. Preserve object visibility through group compositing or multiplicative alpha, then inspect reveal boundaries
- Product handoff: crossfading complete worlds produced duplicate prisms and screens. Transform the actual object where continuity is intended, or use a deliberate clean cut; overlapping copies do not preserve identity
- Product playback: the preview changed before its playhead reached the matching clip. Derive both from one editorial source time and recheck just before/on/after clip boundaries
- Ink ending: numerical boat deceleration did not visibly conclude the story. Docking contact, resting oar and relation to the destination must be readable in the encoded ending

The useful lesson is to repair the visible cause and re-render its affected interval. Do not substitute another universal motion percentage, score threshold or effect quota.

## Research-only proposals, not shipped guarantees

The surveyed projects suggest executable object-bound predicates, shaped-text collision audits, source-claim compilation, dependency-aware partial rebuilds, semantic audio assembly and a richer scene graph. These are not implemented merely because their ideas appear in the ledger. Neither the current text declaration nor the structural shot validator automatically detects text collisions, semantic continuity, incorrect facts, stale assets or every blank frame.

Projective Canvas art is 2.5D without a z-buffer. A separate browser adapter remains experimental and its local verification is blocked by a Unix-socket permission error. A Blender CPU render probe succeeded; a reusable Blender full-film implementation is not the default or a verified shipped route. Do not silently redirect production to either route.

## Evidence boundary

The research contains 79 mechanism references, 3 case-study/tutorial references, 1 mixed reference and 17 controls. All media conclusions are sampled-image observations, with variable coverage recorded per work. No full-speed viewing, listening, 100-project execution, generation-model provenance, cost advantage or parity benchmark is established.
